How to Lower Your Electricity Bill in an Apartment: 8 Easy Ways

Updated 2026-10-06
Advertisement

You do not need to own your home to save on power. Most savings in an apartment come from habits and small plug-in upgrades.

1. Swap bulbs for LEDs

The U.S. Department of Energy says LEDs use at least 75% less energy than incandescent bulbs and last far longer. Bulbs are renter-friendly: take them with you when you move.

2. Use smart power strips

Plug your TV, console and sound system into one strip and switch it off at night to stop standby drain.

3. Adjust the thermostat

Turning it back 7-10°F for 8 hours a day can save up to about 10% a year on heating and cooling, per the DOE.

4. Seal drafts

Removable weatherstripping and window film cost little and come off cleanly at move-out.

5. Wash cold, air-dry when you can

Most laundry energy goes to heating water. Modern detergents work well in cold water.

6. Close blinds in summer, open them in winter

Free heat control: block midday sun when cooling, let it in when heating.

7. Clean or replace HVAC and dryer filters

Clogged filters make appliances work harder and use more power.

8. Compare your electricity plan

If your area has a choice of suppliers, comparing plans once a year can matter more than any gadget.
