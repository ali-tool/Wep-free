Best Thermostat Settings for Winter to Save Money

Updated 2026-10-06
Advertisement

The U.S. Department of Energy suggests about 68°F while you are awake and at home in winter, and lower when you are asleep or away.

A simple schedule

  • Awake at home: around 68°F
  • Asleep: 62-66°F with a warm blanket
  • Away all day: turn it back 7-10°F

Tips

A programmable or smart thermostat automates the schedule. Avoid big jumps: constantly cranking the heat does not warm the room faster.

If you have a heat pump, follow the manufacturer's guidance, as aggressive setbacks can trigger inefficient backup heat.
