Vampire Power: Which Devices Waste Electricity When Turned Off?

Updated 2026-10-06
Advertisement

Vampire power (or standby power) is electricity used by devices that are switched off but still plugged in.

Common culprits

  • Game consoles left in standby or instant-on mode
  • Cable and satellite boxes
  • TVs and soundbars
  • Phone and laptop chargers left in the wall
  • Printers and desktop computers

What to leave plugged in

Your refrigerator, router (if you work from home) and anything with a clock you rely on.

Easy fix

Group entertainment gear on one power strip and turn it off when you leave or sleep. Check console settings and choose a true low-power or off mode.
