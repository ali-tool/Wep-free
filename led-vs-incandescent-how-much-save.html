LED vs Incandescent Bulbs: How Much Can You Really Save?

Updated 2026-10-06
Advertisement

LED bulbs cost more upfront but use much less power and last much longer.

Estimate your own savings

Savings per year = (old watts − new watts) × hours per day × 365 ÷ 1000 × your price per kWh.

Example: replacing a 60W bulb with a 9W LED used 4 hours a day saves about 75 kWh a year. Multiply by your local rate to see the dollars.

Buying tips

  • Choose by lumens (brightness), not watts.
  • 2700K is warm white; 5000K is daylight.
  • Check dimmer compatibility before buying.
